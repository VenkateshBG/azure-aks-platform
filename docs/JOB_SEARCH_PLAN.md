# Job-switch plan (4.5 LPA → 10+ LPA)

I can't promise an outcome; this plan maximises your odds. The biggest lever is **volume of good applications plus a repo you can defend**.

## Week plan

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Update resume + LinkedIn + Naukri headline: "DevOps / Cloud Engineer | Azure, Terraform, Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD". Add the project as "in progress, repo link". Turn on "open to work" for recruiters only. Set job alerts: *DevOps Engineer, Cloud Engineer, Azure DevOps, SRE, Platform Engineer, Azure Administrator* — locations Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Remote. |
| **Days 1–5** | Build and record the project. Apply to **10 roles/day** (50 by the end of the week) — mix of product companies, GCCs, MNC service companies and startups. |
| **Day 5** | Replace "in progress" with the finished repo + demo video + 4 screenshots on the resume. |
| **Weeks 2–8** | 5–10 applications/day, referrals first. Practise the 2-minute project pitch and the 30 questions in `LEARNING_AND_INTERVIEW_PREP.md`. Expect rounds: screening → technical (Linux/networking/Docker/K8s/Terraform/CI) → scenario/troubleshooting → HR. |

## Where the odds are best

- **Referrals** beat cold applications by a wide margin. Message 5 people/day at target companies (ex-colleagues, college alumni, DevOps community groups): 3 lines — who you are, the repo link, "is your team hiring? Could you refer me?"
- **Recruiters on LinkedIn/Naukri:** search "Azure DevOps hiring", "Terraform Kubernetes" and message hiring managers and recruiters with the repo link.
- **GCCs (global capability centres)** and Azure-partner/MSP firms hire Azure + Terraform + K8s profiles regularly and often pay well above 10 LPA for 3+ years.
- Sort out **notice period** now: the shorter it is, the more offers you can convert before year end. If yours is long, ask HR about buy-out/early release and say upfront what you can negotiate.

## Certification (cheap, high-signal for Indian hiring filters)

- **AZ-104 (Azure Administrator)** is the most requested. Microsoft Learn is free; exam ≈ ₹4,000–5,000 (check pricing; student/event vouchers sometimes exist).
- **AZ-400** (DevOps Engineer Expert) requires AZ-104 or AZ-204 first. Start AZ-104 after the project; schedule the exam in 3–4 weeks.
- **CKA** (Kubernetes) carries a lot of weight with your skill set once you have time.

## Salary conversation

- Your current 4.5 LPA is **not** an anchor. Say "I'm looking for roles in the X–Y range based on the market for my skills" — don't volunteer the current figure first; if forced, give it and add that the new role is a different scope.
- Check market ranges for your city and skills on AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and LinkedIn Salary before quoting; use ranges and aim for the upper half. A realistic target for 3 years of experience with this profile is **10–15 LPA**, higher at product companies/GCCs, but it varies a lot by company and how interviews go.
- Never accept at the first number without asking for time; competing offers are the best negotiation tool, so keep interviews going in parallel.

## Interview readiness checklist

- [ ] Can draw the architecture on a whiteboard from memory
- [ ] Can explain every Terraform file, every pipeline step, every K8s field in `deployment.yaml`
- [ ] Linux basics: processes, systemd, journalctl, disk, permissions, `ss`, `curl`, `dig`
- [ ] Networking basics: DNS, TCP handshake, load balancer vs reverse proxy, subnets/CIDR, NSG
- [ ] Git: rebase vs merge, resolving conflicts, branching strategy
- [ ] A troubleshooting story: "pods Pending because cluster out of memory — here's how I diagnosed it (`kubectl describe node`, `top`) and fixed it by right-sizing requests" (it will probably really happen to you)
